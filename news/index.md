# Changelog

## libcmaesr 0.1.0

- Initial CRAN release.
- Lightweight interface to the bundled ‘libcmaes’ C++ library:
  [`cmaes()`](https://libcmaesr.mlr-org.com/reference/cmaes.md),
  [`cmaes_control()`](https://libcmaesr.mlr-org.com/reference/cmaes_control.md)
  and `cmaes_algos`.
- Supports the active, separable and VD variants of CMA-ES as well as
  the IPOP and BIPOP restart strategies.
